01 — The Problem

Self-service laundromats run on thin margins and blind meters.

Under TNB's tariff structure, a facility that lets too many dryers spike at once gets billed a Maximum Demand surcharge which is a cost the operator only discovers after the bill arrives. Purpose-built energy hardware is priced for factories, not a 6–10 machine laundromat, so most SMEs simply operate blind. SmartFlux's bet: the aggregate meter reading they already have is enough, if the software behind it is smart enough to decompose it.

02 — How It Works

One meter reading, disaggregated into five decisions.

EDIS applies signature analysis and tariff logic on top of the raw utility feed — a software intelligence layer, not a device.

Observation

Live view of kW draw, Max Demand headroom, and the active TNB 2025 Time-of-Use zone.

Mitigation

Prescriptive alerts naming exactly how many dryer units to pause before a surcharge triggers.

Optimization

Shifts high-energy cycles into off-peak windows (10 PM–8 AM, weekends) automatically.

Sustainability

Converts saved load into CO₂ avoided using Malaysia's grid emission factor, benchmarked regionally.

Control

FluxAI — a proactive assistant that watches limits and recommends the next operational move.
